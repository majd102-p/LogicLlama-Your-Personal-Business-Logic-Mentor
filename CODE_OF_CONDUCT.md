# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative community for all contributors.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on the work, not the person.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Assume good intent unless there is evidence otherwise.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or pull requests.
- Abusive, offensive, or exclusionary language.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers through the repository issue tracker or the project contact channels.
